## 🚀 **How It Works Now:**

### **Step 1: Get Image URL**
1. **Upload image** to any hosting service:
   - **Imgur:** Upload at imgur.com → Copy direct image URL
   - **Google Drive:** Upload → Right-click → "Get link" → Change to direct link
   - **Dropbox:** Upload → Right-click → "Copy link" → Change to direct link
   - **Any other service:** Just get the direct image URL

### **Step 2: Paste URL**
1. **Go to:** Destinations → Add Destination → Media tab
2. **Paste URL** in the green "Add Image URL" section
3. **Image preview** appears immediately
4. **Save destination** - Image is included
